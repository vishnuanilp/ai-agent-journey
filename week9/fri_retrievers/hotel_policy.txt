Check-in begins at 2pm and check-out is at 11am. Early check-in may be available on request but is not guaranteed.

Guests may bring pets. They must be under 10kg and are not allowed in the dining area or the pool deck.

Breakfast is served from 7am to 10am in the ground floor restaurant. It is included in the room rate for all bookings made directly with the hotel.

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before arrival are fully refunded. Within 48 hours, one night is charged.

Parking is free for guests in the rear lot. The hotel is not responsible for items left in vehicles.
