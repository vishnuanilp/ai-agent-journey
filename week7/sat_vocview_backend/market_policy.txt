Opening hours: We are open 8am to 9pm Monday to Saturday. We are closed on Sunday.

Location and parking: We are on Main Road, next to the post office. Customer parking is free for the first hour. There are ten spaces at the rear.

Home delivery: We deliver within 5km. Send your list on WhatsApp to 98765 43210 before 4pm and we deliver the same evening. Delivery is free on orders above 500 rupees, otherwise it is 40 rupees.

Payment: We accept cash, UPI, and all major cards. We do not accept cheques.

Returns: Packaged goods can be returned within 7 days with the bill. Fresh items such as vegetables, fruit, milk and meat cannot be returned once they leave the shop.

Loyalty card: The card is free at the counter. You earn 1 point for every 100 rupees spent. 200 points gives you a 100 rupee discount. Points expire after one year.

Fresh stock: Vegetables and fruit arrive daily at 7am. Fish arrives Tuesday and Friday mornings only.

Bulk orders: For orders above 10,000 rupees, contact the manager at least two days in advance for a discount.
